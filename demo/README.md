## What was removed

Patient name, UHID and every other hospital number, phone, address, relatives' names, bed number, the names and signatures of every doctor and staff member, QR codes and barcodes, and the photos' hidden metadata. Each one is covered with random blocks, so nothing underneath can be recovered. The PDF is rebuilt from images, so it has no hidden text layer.

Kept, because the case makes no sense without them: age and sex, the clinical findings, and the clinical dates (the lab table is organised by date).

## Notes on the video

- The app screens and the typing are animated recreations made for this video. They follow the real app's steps, but they are not a screen recording. In real use the AI writes the whole draft in one go, in about 3 minutes.
- The pages shown are this case's real photos. The document shown is the draft the system really made for this case; nothing in it was changed except covering identifiers.
- The usage numbers in the video come from the live usage register, as of 16 October 2026.
